## 틱톡 (직접 게시 · reel.mp4)
```
Blush nails looking patchy? You're probably painting the colour on. Korean blush nails are dabbed, not painted 💅

Here's the at-home version, no airbrush needed:

✂️ Shape first: short and softly rounded, just past the fingertip. A soft tip makes the flush look natural.
🤍 Base: 1–2 thin coats of a sheer milky pink (milky or "syrup" polishes work well). Wait about a minute so it sets.
🧽 Flush: put ONE small drop of peach, rose or berry polish on a makeup sponge, not on the nail. Dab off the extra on paper first.
👆 Tap it into the centre of the nail and work outward with light taps. Stop before the edges so they stay sheer.
✨ Finish: a glossy top coat, swiped along the tip too. The shine smooths the gradient into one soft look.

🤔 Why it works: the colour is strongest in the middle and fades out, just like cheek blush. That's why it looks soft, not stripy.

🚫 Common mistake: too much polish on the sponge. You get a hard blotch instead of a flush. Less is more, you can always add a second tap.

🍂 For fall, try berry over the milky base.

🔖 Save this for your next nail night.
```
