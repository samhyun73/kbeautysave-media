## 틱톡 (직접 게시 · reel.mp4)
```
Dark nails look heavy on you? Try the Korean "wine nails" way 🍷

💅 1. Shape first. File them short, just past the fingertip, with softly rounded corners. Dark colour makes every millimetre of length look bigger, so short tips keep it neat.
🧴 2. Thin base coat. Paint it on the nail only and leave a hair-thin gap at the skin. Polish that touches the skin is what makes dark nails look messy.
🍷 3. Two thin colour coats. A sheer burgundy, plum or mocha builds depth slowly. Wait a minute or two between coats so each one sets.
✨ 4. Glossy top coat, then cap the tip. Swipe the brush along the free edge of the nail too. Shine is what makes deep shades look fresh instead of flat.
🖌️ 5. Clean up. Dip a small flat brush in remover and trace the edge for a sharp line.

Why it works: Korean nail looks favour short, glossy and slightly sheer, so even burgundy reads polished, not heavy.

Common mistake: one thick coat to "save time". It stays soft longer and the edges look bulky.

Save this for your next nail day 🔖
```
