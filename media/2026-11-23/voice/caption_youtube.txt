Cat eye nails look flat? It's almost always the magnet timing, not the polish. How to do cat eye nails at home, step by step:
1. Paint one nail with a thin coat, then use the magnet right away while the polish is wet.
2. Hold the magnet just above the nail for about 10 seconds. Close, but never touching.
3. For velvet nails, sweep the magnet slowly from side to side instead of holding it still.
Common mistake: painting all ten nails first and using the magnet at the end. The polish has started to set and the shine can't move.

Wet polish moves. Set polish doesn't.
