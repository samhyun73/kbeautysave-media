How to do cat eye nails at home, without the flat, muddy look 💅
It's almost always the magnet timing, not the polish.

🖌️ Paint ONE nail with a thin coat, then pick up the magnet right away while it's wet
🧲 Hold it just above the nail for about 10 seconds. Close, never touching
✨ For a soft velvet look, sweep the magnet slowly side to side instead

The common mistake: painting all ten nails first and using the magnet at the end. By then the polish has started to set and the shine can't move.

Wet polish moves. Set polish doesn't.
