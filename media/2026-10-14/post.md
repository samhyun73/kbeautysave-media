Asking for "layers" at the salon and getting a choppy cut? A Korean layered cut (레이어드 컷) is soft because the request is specific. Here's exactly what to say ✂️

📸 Bring two photos, one from the front and one from the back. Pick hair with a texture close to yours (fine, thick, wavy), because the same cut looks very different on different hair.
📏 Name where the first layer starts. Say "shortest layer at my chin." If you want more face framing, say "at my cheekbone." This one line stops the top layer from going too short.
💇 Protect your length. Say "keep my length, just soften the ends." Ask for light thinning only, especially if your hair is fine.
🌀 Style it at home in about 5 minutes: towel dry by pressing, dry the roots first with the nozzle pointing down, then roll a round brush under the ends and give it a few seconds of cool air.
💧 Finish with a few drops of hair oil warmed in your palms, on the ends only, never the roots.

🤔 Why it works: "layers" means something different to every stylist. A start point plus a length rule gives them a clear picture.

🚫 Common mistake: asking for lots of thinning to get movement. On fine hair it leaves the ends see-through. Shape comes from where the layers start.
