Hair mask on, hair still dry and rough after? 💧 Most of the time the mask isn't the problem. The water is.

1️⃣ Squeeze your hair and press it with a towel after shampoo, so it's damp, not dripping
2️⃣ Smooth a small scoop from the middle of your hair down to the ends, keeping it away from the roots
3️⃣ Run a wide-tooth comb through, give it 3 to 5 minutes, rinse with lukewarm water and add a drop or two of oil to the ends

🚫 Skip the dripping-wet step. Extra water dilutes the mask and it rinses away.

Less water, more mask ✨
