How to use a hair mask so it actually does something for dry winter hair 💆‍♀️
It's usually how wet your hair is, not the mask.

🫧 After shampoo, squeeze the water out and press with a towel. Damp, not dripping
🧴 A quarter-sized amount on the mid-lengths to ends, never the scalp
🪮 Comb it through, wait 3 to 5 minutes (or the label time), rinse lukewarm, then 1 or 2 drops of oil on the ends

The common mistake: mask on dripping wet hair. The water thins it out and most of it slides off.

Less water, more mask.
