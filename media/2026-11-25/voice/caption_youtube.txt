Hair mask not doing much for dry hair? It's usually how wet your hair is, not the mask. How to use a hair mask correctly:
1. After shampoo, squeeze the water out and press with a towel. Damp, not dripping.
2. Use about a quarter-sized amount (more for long hair) on the mid-lengths to ends. Keep it off the scalp.
3. Comb it through with a wide-tooth comb and leave it 3 to 5 minutes, or as long as the label says.
4. Rinse with lukewarm water, then 1 or 2 drops of hair oil on the ends.
Common mistake: putting the mask on dripping wet hair. The water thins it out and most of it slides off.

Less water, more mask.
