Hair flat by lunch? 💇‍♀️ The Korean fix starts with the blow-dry.

How to keep the volume:
• Dry the roots first, brushing them the opposite way from your part, then a few seconds of cool air
• Put a velcro roller at the crown or in your bangs for about 10 minutes while you do your makeup
• A little volume spray or root powder at the roots only, and hair oil on the ends

The common mistake: conditioner or oil close to the scalp. It weighs the roots down within a few hours.
