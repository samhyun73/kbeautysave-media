Does your hair go flat by noon? 💇‍♀️ In Korea, root volume starts with how you dry it.

1️⃣ Dry your roots before the ends, brushing them the opposite way from your part. Finish with a few seconds of cool air
2️⃣ Roll a velcro roller at your crown or into your bangs and leave it about 10 minutes while you do your makeup
3️⃣ Mist a little volume spray, or tap root powder, at the roots only. Hair oil goes on the ends
🚫 Conditioner or oil near the scalp weighs the roots down by lunch.
