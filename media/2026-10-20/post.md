Does your face feel tight a few minutes after washing? 😣 That squeaky feeling is not "extra clean". It usually means the wash was harsher than your skin needed. Here's the gentle Korean way:

💧 Lukewarm water. Aim for about body temperature, warm enough to feel nice but never hot. Hot water lifts off more of the skin's own oils, which is a big reason skin feels pulled afterwards.
🫧 A pea-sized gel cleanser. Pick a gentle, low pH gel cleanser and lather it in your wet palms first, so you're not rubbing foam straight onto your face.

🤲 Pat, don't rub. Press a clean towel on your face and leave the skin a little damp.
🧴 Toner within a minute. Pat a hydrating toner on while skin is still damp, then your usual moisturizer.

🤔 Why it works: damp skin takes toner and moisturizer in more easily, and a gentle wash leaves the skin feeling soft instead of stripped.
