Korean beauty word of the day: mul-gwang (물광) 💧 "Water glow", that dewy, just-hydrated shine.

How to get the look:
• Layer a hydrating toner 2-3 times on damp skin, patting each thin layer in
• Add one drop of facial oil to your moisturizer and press it in with your palms
• Finish with a dewy cushion foundation and a touch of balm on the high points of your face

The common mistake: shine everywhere. Keep it on the cheekbones and nose bridge so it reads glowy, not greasy.
