1. Pat a hydrating toner into damp skin in 2-3 thin layers.
2. Mix one drop of facial oil into your moisturizer and press it in with your palms.
3. Use a dewy cushion foundation and tap a little balm on your cheekbones and nose bridge.
Common mistake: shine all over the face. Keep it on the high points.
