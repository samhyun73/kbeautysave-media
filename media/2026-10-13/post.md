Still swiping toner on a cotton pad? In Korea, toner goes on by hand, in thin layers. It's often called the 7 skin method, but you don't need seven 💧

Here's how to do it tonight:

🫧 Start right after cleansing, while your skin is still a little damp. Pat dry with a towel, don't rub.
💧 Pour a coin-sized amount of toner into one palm, then rub your palms together for a second so both hands are covered.
👐 Press your palms flat on your cheeks for about 5 seconds, then forehead and chin. Finish with light taps with your fingertips.
⏳ Wait 20–30 seconds, until your skin feels a little tacky, not wet.
🔁 Repeat with another thin layer. Three layers is plenty for most skin. Then finish with your usual moisturizer to seal it in.

🤔 Why hands? A cotton pad soaks up a lot of the toner before it reaches your face, and swiping adds friction. Thin layers pressed in feel more hydrating than one heavy splash.

🚫 Common mistake: layering an exfoliating toner (AHA, BHA or other acids) this way. Keep the layering for a gentle hydrating toner and use acid toners once, on their own days.
