How to make curls last all day, even through a holiday party 💇‍♀️
Curls gone flat before you even get there? It's not your hair. It's the minute after the iron.

🔥 Fully dry hair, small sections about 2 fingers wide
⏱️ Hold each curl ~5 seconds, then catch it in your palm
📌 Pin it flat and let it cool about a minute, then loosen with your fingers and mist hairspray from arm's length

🚫 The common mistake: brushing curls out while they're still warm. Warm hair still moves, so the curl drops.

Heat shapes the curl. Cooling keeps it ✨
