Party curls that fall flat before the party starts? 💇‍♀️ Usually the curling isn't the problem. What you do right after is.

1️⃣ Start with completely dry hair and work in small sections, two fingers wide
2️⃣ Hold the iron about 5 seconds, then let the curl roll into your hand
3️⃣ Clip it to your head for about a minute until it's cool, then break it up with your fingers and add a light mist of hairspray from arm's length

🚫 Skip the brush while the curls are still warm. Warm hair hasn't set yet, so the curl slides out.

Heat shapes the curl, cooling keeps it ✨
