Curls gone flat before the party even starts? It's not your hair. It's the minute after the iron. How to make curls last all day:
1. Curl fully dry hair (heat protectant first) in small sections, about two fingers wide.
2. Hold each curl about 5 seconds, then catch it in your palm instead of letting it drop.
3. Pin it flat to your head and let it cool for about a minute.
4. Once it's cool, loosen it with your fingers and mist hairspray from arm's length.
Common mistake: brushing curls out while they're still warm. Warm hair still moves, so the curl drops.

Heat shapes the curl. Cooling keeps it.
