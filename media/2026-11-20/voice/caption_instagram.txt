How to paint your nails perfectly at home, without polish on your cuticles 💅
It's usually the brush, not your shaky hand.

🖌️ Wipe one side of the brush on the bottle neck. Keep a small bead on the tip
💅 Set the bead mid-nail, a hair from the cuticle. One stroke down the center, one down each side
🧽 Before it dries, trace the edge with a small flat brush dipped in remover

The common mistake: a fully loaded brush, then going back over tacky polish. That's what floods the edges.

Less polish on the brush, cleaner lines on the nail.
