Polish all over your cuticles? It's usually how much is on the brush, not your hand. How to paint your nails perfectly at home, the Korean way:
1. Wipe one side of the brush on the bottle neck. Keep a small bead on the tip.
2. Set the bead mid-nail, a hair from the cuticle. One stroke down the center, one down each side.
3. Before it dries, trace the edge with a small flat brush dipped in remover.
Common mistake: a fully loaded brush, then going back over tacky polish. That floods the edges.

Less polish on the brush, cleaner lines on the nail.
