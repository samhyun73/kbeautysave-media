Polish ends up on your cuticles every time? 💅 Here's how to paint your nails neatly at home, the Korean way.

1️⃣ Wipe the brush on one side of the bottle neck so only a small bead is left
2️⃣ Place it mid-nail, just shy of the cuticle. Center stroke, then one on each side. Three strokes and you're done
3️⃣ Clean the edge before it dries: a small flat brush and a little remover

🚫 Skip the overloaded brush, and don't go back over polish that has turned tacky.
