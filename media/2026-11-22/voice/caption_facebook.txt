How to use a face mist without your skin feeling drier afterwards 💦
It's not the mist. It's what happens in the next 10 seconds.

📏 Spray 2 or 3 times from about an arm's length, eyes closed
🤲 Press it in with clean palms right away, before it dries on its own
🧴 Finish with a thin layer of cream (a little balm on any dry patches)

Most common mistake: misting over and over through the day without sealing it. Heated rooms dry it off fast.

Mist is a first step, not the last one.
