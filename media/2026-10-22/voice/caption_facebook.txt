Blush gone by lunch? 🌸 The Korean fix is layering, not more product.

How to make it last:
• Give your sunscreen and base 2 minutes to settle. Blush slides around on damp skin
• Tap a cream or liquid blush onto your cheeks with your fingertip
• Set it with a light dusting of powder blush in the same shade

The common mistake: one thick layer of powder. Two thin layers hold up better through the day.
