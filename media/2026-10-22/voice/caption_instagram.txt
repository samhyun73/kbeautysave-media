Why does your blush disappear by noon? 🌸 It's usually how you layer it.

1️⃣ Let sunscreen and base sit for 2 minutes first
2️⃣ Tap a cream or liquid blush on with your fingertip. Tap, don't swipe
3️⃣ Dust a little powder blush in the same shade on top
🚫 One heavy layer of powder wears off unevenly. Two thin layers stay put longer.
