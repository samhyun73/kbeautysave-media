Why does your blush disappear by noon? It's usually how you layer it, not the blush itself. The Korean way:
1. Let your sunscreen and base sit for 2 minutes. Blush slides around on damp skin.
2. Tap a cream or liquid blush onto your cheeks with your fingertip. Tap, don't swipe.
3. Dust a little powder blush in the same shade on top. Two thin layers stay put longer.
Common mistake: one heavy layer of powder. It sits on top and wears off unevenly.
