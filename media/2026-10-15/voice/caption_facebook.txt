Why does lip tint go patchy halfway through the day? Most of the time it's dry lips 💋

The Korean gradient-lip routine:
• A thin layer of lip balm, then blot it off after a minute
• Tint only on the inner half of the lips
• Pat it outward with your fingertip for a soft edge

The common mistake: outlining the whole lip first. That line is the part that wears off unevenly.
