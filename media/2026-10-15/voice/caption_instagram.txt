Lip tint looking patchy by lunch? 💄 It's usually dry lips, not the tint.

1️⃣ A thin layer of lip balm, wait one minute, then blot with a tissue
2️⃣ Tap the tint on the inner half of your lips only
3️⃣ Blend outward with a fingertip so the edge fades softly
🚫 Skip lining the whole lip first. That hard edge is what wears off unevenly.
