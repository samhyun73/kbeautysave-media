Winged liner making your eyes look sharp or tired? Korean "puppy eyeliner" (강아지 눈매) does the opposite: the tail goes DOWN, so the eye looks rounder and softer. Here's exactly how 🐶

✏️ Start halfway. Use a brown pencil or gel liner and draw a thin line only on the outer half of your upper lash line, right in the lashes. Brown reads softer than black on camera and in daylight.
📐 Angle the tail down. Look straight into the mirror and follow the direction of your lower lashes out past the corner, about 3 mm. Keep it short and thin; a long tail starts to look like a wing again.
🔺 Close the triangle. Draw a short line from the end of the tail back to your upper lid so you get a tiny triangle, then fill it with a matte brown shadow using a small flat brush. Skip shimmer here, it makes the shape look smudgy in a messy way.
☁️ Smudge the tail. Run a cotton bud along the end once or twice so there's no hard edge.
👀 Optional: a little of the same brown under the outer third of the lower lashes ties it together.

🤔 Why it works: a line that dips at the outer corner makes the eye look rounder and gentler, which is the look behind 강아지 눈매.

🚫 Common mistake: following the upper lid crease up out of habit. Check your tail with your eyes open, not closed.
