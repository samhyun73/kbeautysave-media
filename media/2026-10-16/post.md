Halloween nails that don't look like a costume 🎃 Korean nail style keeps it sheer, smoky and a little spooky, so you can wear it all week. Here's exactly how to do it at home:

🤍 Start with a milky base. One or two thin coats of a sheer milky or pinky white polish. Let it set for about a minute between coats.
🖤 Make it smoky, not solid. Wipe most of the black polish off the brush on the bottle neck, then do ONE thin coat. You should still see the milky nail underneath. That see-through look is what makes it "jelly".
🎨 Pick one dark and one light shade. Smoky black or plum for most nails, then sheer pumpkin or a shimmery cream for contrast. Two colours is plenty.
🌙 Add one accent nail. A tiny moon or star on the ring finger only. A nail sticker is easiest, or use a fine detail brush and keep the shape smaller than a grain of rice.
💅 Keep them short and rounded, just past the fingertip, then finish with a glossy top coat. Wait 10 minutes before touching anything.

🤔 Why it works: sheer dark colour plus shine reads like coloured glass instead of flat paint, and short tips keep it cute instead of witchy.

🚫 Common mistake: two or three thick coats of black. It goes opaque and flat, chips faster, and every brush stroke shows.
