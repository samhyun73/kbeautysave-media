## What the winners share
Small sample (5 FB reels, differences of ±35 views), so treat as hints, not rules.
- **Hook style:** the two "you're doing it wrong" hooks won: "Your routine order might be backwards" got the most views, "You're washing your face wrong" held people longest. The plain curiosity question ("Why is cica on every label?") had the shortest watch time.
- **Topic type:** routine/how-to (layering, double cleansing) beat ingredient explainers (cica). Culture (glass skin) sat in the middle.
- **Length:** all reels are the same template (6 frames, ~17.5 s), so length did not differentiate this week. Caption length did not either (the shortest caption, 09-29, had the best watch time but fewer views).
- Engagement is near zero everywhere (2 FB likes total). Views come from distribution, not from the audience yet.
