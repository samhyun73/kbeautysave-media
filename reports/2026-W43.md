## What the winners share
Small sample (two big voice winners so far); hints, not rules.
- **Format:** narrated stock-footage voice Shorts won on FB and IG this week (IG 107–111 vs 10–18; FB 6.9 s vs 1.7 s). On YouTube only the blush one broke out; flat roots (126) and mul-gwang (96) did no better than the slide Shorts (114–165).
- **Hook style:** both ~1k YouTube winners (10-15 lip tint 1,230, 10-22 blush 973) open with **a visible everyday makeup problem + when it happens**, then a **relief line that takes the blame off the viewer's product** ("It's usually dry lips, not the tint." / "It's usually how you layer it, not the blush itself."). Flat roots had the problem + time cue but its second line ("root volume starts with how you dry it") had no "not your X" relief, and it was hair. Mul-gwang opened with a definition, no problem.
- **Topic type:** makeup = the two winners (also the sales category). Hair = mid reach (non-buying markets, LEARNINGS 10-21). Skincare-glow word-of-day = lowest.
- **Length:** the 40 s blush Short was the longest and held best; length did not hurt. What matters is the first 3 s.
- Engagement: still 0 comments everywhere, including the two Shorts that ended with a comment question ("Which step do you skip?", "Do you use rollers?"). The question did not create comments.

## Suggestions for this week (W44, within the rules)
1. **Voice Shorts (Tue/Wed/Thu, human-present): lead with makeup/base problems in the winning 2-line shape, but rotate the hook formula** (the "…by noon" frame was used 10-21 and 10-22, so don't use it a third time). Examples outside the last 30 topics:
   - Tue: "Cushion foundation looking cakey? It's usually the amount on the puff, not the cushion." (쿠션이 들떠 보이나요? 보통 쿠션 탓이 아니라 퍼프에 묻힌 양 때문이에요.) Steps: moisturizer settles ~2 min, press the puff once, tap from the centre of the face outward, second thin layer only where needed; mistake: swiping/dragging the puff.
   - Thu: "You're probably putting concealer on too thick." (컨실러, 너무 두껍게 바르고 계실 거예요.) Steps: a tiny dot only where it's dark, tap with a ring finger, a little powder pressed on top; mistake: a big triangle under the eye that creases.
   Emotion `relief` for both; close on a shareable line (e.g. "Less on the puff, more on the face." / 퍼프엔 적게, 얼굴엔 얇게). Copy rules: no code or link in voice Shorts, copy points to the profile, `#oliveyoungaffiliate #ad`, Coverr credit when used, no "I tried".

Steps: sheer base + no-wipe top coat, a tiny bit of powder rubbed in with a fingertip or silicone tool, brush off, top coat.
