## What the winners share
Small sample; hints, not rules.
- **Topic type:** nails and makeup won reach (wine nails, blush, jelly nails = the 3 best topics on FB + YT combined). Hair (10-07) was lowest on FB and YouTube; skincare (Halloween removal) was middle on FB, near zero on YouTube. That matches the Pinterest-volume direction set on 10-03.
- **Hook style:** W40 said "you're doing it wrong" > curiosity question. W41 doesn't confirm it: the best watch time came from a curiosity question ("Why do Korean nails look so clean?") and the best reach from a mistake hook (blush) and a relatable-problem question (wine nails). What the top three share is a **visual, specific subject named in the first words** (nails, blush) rather than the hook grammar.
- **Length / pace:** shorter, faster `bright` reels held people ~0.8 s longer (see above). Frame count was 6 in every reel; only the BPM/length differed.
- **YouTube:** strongly uneven (11–183). Nails/makeup shorts got 117–183; hair and skincare 11–15. Title hook and on-screen hook matched every day, so topic is the likeliest driver.
- Engagement is still near zero everywhere (FB 0 likes, IG 0, YT 7). Views come from distribution, not from an audience yet.

## Suggestions for this week (W42, within the rules)
1. **Test `mood: "bright"` on every weekday reel this week** (shorter 96-bpm cut, ~14.8 s, same 6 frames). It's an existing spec option, no tool change. If the FB weighted avg watch reaches ≥ 2.0 s again with mixed topics, make bright the default (proposal for a human-present session).
