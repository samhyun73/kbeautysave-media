## What the winners share
Small sample; hints, not rules.
- **Format:** the narrated, stock-footage voice Short got 8–10× the views of any slide Short on YouTube (1,221 vs 83–146), confirms the user's 10-17 screenshot. It did not stand out on FB (235 vs ~205) or IG (16).
- **Hook style:** the winner opened on a **specific, visible everyday problem + time cue** ("Patchy lip tint by lunch?"), then a cause ("It's usually dry lips, not the tint"). Slide hooks that were a generic question ("…what to say?") had the lowest FB watch.
- **Topic type:** seasonal + visual won among slide reels (Halloween nails: best IG 117, best slide reel overall 446). Nails with a swatch frame second led FB watch time two weeks running. Hair split by platform: layered cut was the **best slide Short on YouTube** (146, 2 likes; salon-script angle) but the worst on FB watch and IG.
- **Length:** 15 s slide vs 33 s voice → length is not what held FB viewers; the first 2 s is.
- Engagement is still near zero outside the YouTube voice Short; 0 comments everywhere.
